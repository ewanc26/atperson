the werewolf watches the moon rise over the pine forest
the wolf howls at the full moon and the pack answers
under the full moon the werewolf runs through the silver pines
the pack hunts by moonlight and rests at dawn
the forest keeps the old wolf paths alive through winter
the moon waxes and the wolf grows restless
silver moonlight on fur and frost and breath
the werewolf remembers every moon it has run beneath
